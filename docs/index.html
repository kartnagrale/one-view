What Problem Are We Solving?

Before AgriAnalytix, teams had to check multiple systems manually to understand what was happening in our markets.

😵

Too Many Systems

Market data, Sugar data, and NCDFI data lived in separate places. No single view of the entire business.

⏱️

Slow Decision Making

Teams waited hours or days for trade reports and price trends. By then, the market had already moved.

🗺️

No Geographic View

We couldn't easily see which states were most active, which commodities were trading, and where prices were highest.

✅ AgriAnalytix solves all three problems in one unified platform

What Is AgriAnalytix?

Think of it as a Smart Control Room for our agricultural business — showing everything that's happening right now.

📈

Live Price Tracking

See today's commodity prices, whether they went up or down, and how they compare to last month.

🏆

Auction Results

Track which auctions were completed, how many were approved, and the total trade value generated.

🗺️

India State Map

See on a map of India which states are trading the most — click any state to filter the data instantly.

🤖

AI Assistant

Ask questions in plain language like "Which commodity had the best price this month?" and get instant answers.

Key Features at a Glance

Everything a business manager or market analyst needs — in one screen.

🎯

Live Price Cards

See current prices for each commodity. Green = price went up. Red = price went down. Simple colour coding.

📉

Monthly Trend Graph

Bar chart shows Opening, Highest, Lowest, and Closing prices each month — easy to spot patterns.

🥇

Top Rankings

See which clients and commodities contributed the most trade value. Updated automatically.

⚖️

Buy vs Sell Auctions

Separate view for Selling Auctions (Forward) and Buying Auctions (Reverse). Understand market direction.

📦

Quantity by Unit

Breakdown by MT, KG, Bags — never mixed together. Accurate totals per measurement type.

📤

Export to Excel

Download any view as an Excel file for further analysis or sharing with your team.
